# Evidence standard for avoidable delay

Milestone 4 selected outcome C. This defines further evidence requirements, not a redesigned workflow, approved policy or intervention proposal.

A long interval is a location to investigate. It becomes verified avoidable delay only when feasible earlier action, responsibility, timing and consequence are supported. A necessary prerequisite does not prove all elapsed time necessary; lack of comments does not prove inactivity.

## Required evidence record

| Required fact | Evidence to obtain | Current limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Actual reason and reason changes | Dated dependency history, artifact specification, failed test or approval condition | One Waiting label can span several blockers. |
| Prerequisite completion / ready-to-act time | Relevant logs received and accepted, environment ready, approval granted, test accepted, change window available | “Uploaded” can refer to unsuitable material; readiness is not directly logged. |
| Responsible and feasible actor | Accountable role, authority, access, skills, availability and competing commitments | Individual assignment is not a team, queue, schedule or capacity record. |
| Reasonable response expectation | Process-owner-confirmed policy or explicit commitment, applicable calendar, exceptions and rationale | Seven calendar days and reminder templates are not verified standards. |
| Actual next action and remaining blockers | Timestamped work/change/approval evidence, not only status changes | Technical work occurs within Waiting; exit may lag action or restoration. |
| Complete communication chain | Comments, relevant calls/messages, linked development work and successor tickets with appropriate access | Sampled text references off-platform communications and new tickets. |
| Independent operational consequence | Verified restoration, user impact period, missed commitment, repeated contact, failed deployment or measured effort | Resolution/closure can represent nonresponse or administration. |
| Credible earlier-action comparison | Comparable ready episodes, justified standard or prospective evidence of feasible earlier action with equivalent safeguards | Fastest peers and duration differences confound case mix and legitimate dependencies. |

## Adjudication categories

1. **Necessary dependency documented:** a specific blocking prerequisite is evidenced. Its completion time and expected duration may remain unknown; do not label the entire interval necessary.
2. **Potentially addressable gap:** a plausible actor and feasible next action are identified after a prerequisite appears satisfied, but availability, expected timing, other blockers or harm remain unverified.
3. **Verified avoidable operational delay:** an authorized available actor could have taken a specific earlier action after relevant prerequisites were satisfied; an applicable expectation was missed without a substantiated exception; a meaningful consequence and credible earlier-action comparison are supported.
4. **Administrative status lag:** operational action or recovery precedes the state update. Investigate separately; do not count it as unrecovered service or labor savings.
5. **Unknown / disputed:** insufficient, masked, contradictory or incomplete evidence. Preserve this category rather than forcing a label.

An episode can contain segments with different categories. Do not allocate an entire case duration to one reason. Record evidence IDs, timestamps, rationale, competing explanations and disagreement. Distinguish human adjudication from model suggestions. This project currently has no stakeholder validation.

## Evidence collection and selection gate

Use a consecutive or probability-based operational sample with known inclusion rules, covering long and short waits, repeated and single waits, and relevant projects/time periods. Deliberate examples clarify mechanisms but cannot estimate population burden. Determine sample size from prevalence, uncertainty and the decision; do not promise significance from an arbitrary small number.

Have actual process owners confirm state semantics, responsibilities and exceptions. Obtain independent review of the proposed mechanism and disagreements. Test necessary-wait explanations and keep unresolved cases unknown. Verify historical availability before treating priority, project, reason or text as a prospective predictor.

Select **A** only if a recurring, materially harmful, controllable mechanism within Waiting is established for a defined population with reliable measurement. Select **B** only if another problem has stronger evidence on those criteria, not merely a convenient transition. Otherwise retain **C** and obtain better evidence or explicitly change the project to a bounded observational study.

Only after this gate should process redesign, deterministic rules, traditional automation or AI be evaluated. A later pilot or causal evaluation must preserve necessary validation and operational safeguards. No savings or treatment effects can be calculated from the present review.
