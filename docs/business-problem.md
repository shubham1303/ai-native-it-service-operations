# Business-problem decision after Milestone 4

**Outcome C: the available data is insufficient to establish an addressable operational problem.** No core business problem or intervention has been selected. This supersedes Milestone 3's provisional prioritization of long/repeated Waiting and its early intervention comparison framework.

The measured observation remains valid: Waiting occupies 41.96% of recorded status time, and 1,299 cases (7.58%) contribute 25.18% of Waiting time. Review shows that Waiting combines technical dependencies, testing, coordination, investigation and administrative closure. Concentration does not establish removability or harm.

Validation returns frequently precede Waiting, but explain only a subset of repeat-wait gaps. The exact transition also occurs after successful testing when work moves into production. It is not a verified rework label and does not justify replacing Waiting as the business problem.

The strongest narrower lead is **diagnostic-information and dependency coordination**. Wrong or incomplete logs, extraction/access constraints and changing prerequisites are directly evidenced in selected cases. Their representative burden, controllability, feasible earlier actions and adverse consequences remain unverified.

The next validation question is:

> When diagnostic information or a dependency is ready, does a controllable coordination failure delay a feasible next action and cause a meaningful adverse outcome?

This is a falsifiable question, not an asserted problem. Obtain prerequisite-ready timestamps, accountable actor availability and authority, validated response expectations, complete linked communications, and independent outcome evidence. Preserve necessary waits and unknown cases as comparators.

Read the [Milestone 4 analysis and candidate comparison](problem-validation.md), [review protocol](milestone4-review-protocol.md) and [avoidability evidence standard](avoidability-evidence-standard.md). Process redesign, deterministic rules, traditional automation and AI evaluation remain gated until the business problem is selected and validated. No savings or counterfactual benefits are estimated.
